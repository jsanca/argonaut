# DOC-010 — Chunk boundaries and operational warnings

Chunking changes what a retriever can return. Keep a warning together with the condition that explains it: a sentence ending one chunk and its exception beginning the next can make either chunk insufficient alone. Overlap can preserve nearby context, but excessive overlap creates near-duplicate candidates.

For operational documents, place a heading, the prerequisite, the warning, and the remediation in a bounded section. Evaluate chunk policies with queries that target content around a heading or paragraph boundary, not only short standalone facts.
