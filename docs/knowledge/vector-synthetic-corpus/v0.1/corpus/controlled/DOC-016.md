# DOC-016 — RAG evidence and chunk provenance

Retrieval-augmented generation should retain the source document identifier, chunk identifier, rank, and retrieval score for every passage supplied to a later stage. Provenance makes a result auditable even when the corpus has documents with overlapping language.

When a question requires two facts, retrieve and preserve both sources rather than merging them into an untraceable synthetic note. Retrieval evaluation stops at the ranked evidence list; evaluating a generated answer is a separate task.
